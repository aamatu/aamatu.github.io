---
title: Molecular Tumor Board and translation into practice
weight: 5
---

The Molecular Tumor Board of the Medical Oncology Division at Niguarda is part of the regional network. Its purpose is straightforward: to turn a comprehensive genomic report into a recommendation a treating oncologist can act on. That means matching an alteration to an approved drug, to an open trial or to a compassionate-use request, and being explicit when the best option is standard therapy.

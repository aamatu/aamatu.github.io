Molecularly guided trials with specific treatment strategies for advanced, newly defined molecular subtypes of colorectal cancer. Horizon 2020 project funded by the European Union and coordinated by Vall d'Hebron Institute of Oncology in Barcelona; Niguarda was one of two Italian clinical centres running the trials. Patients were stratified by tumour gene expression profile and matched to the trial most likely to help them, with serial ctDNA monitoring of resistance. I took part in the scientific and coordination meetings, treated patients in the associated studies, and am first author of two and co-author of ten of the resulting publications.

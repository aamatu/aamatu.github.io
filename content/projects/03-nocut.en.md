Liquid biopsy and a gene signature to guide non-operative management of rectal cancer. Prospective multicentre study of total neoadjuvant therapy followed by non-operative management in patients with a complete response, or surgery in the others, assessing tumour response alongside circulating genetic and epigenetic biomarkers and the stromal transcriptome in order to interpret clinical outcome. Funded by Fondazione AIRC per la Ricerca sul Cancro ETS, with the University of Milan as partner. Principal investigator: Salvatore Siena. Primary results published in The Lancet Oncology in 2025.

A window-of-opportunity trial testing whether high-dose vitamin C can enhance neoadjuvant immune checkpoint therapy in mismatch repair-proficient (pMMR/MSS) colon cancer, the setting in which checkpoint inhibitors usually do least. Open-label phase 2 pilot study in two populations: stage II–III pMMR/MSS colon cancer scheduled for upfront surgery, and stage IV liver-limited oligometastatic disease scheduled for surgery on both the primary tumour and the metastasis. Sponsored by Grande Ospedale Metropolitano Niguarda.

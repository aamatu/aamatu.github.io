---
title: Liquid biopsy and ctDNA-guided treatment
weight: 2
---

Plasma genotyping makes it possible to examine a tumour repeatedly without another biopsy. In the phase 2 CHRONOS trial, circulating tumour DNA was used prospectively to decide which patients should be re-treated with panitumumab after progression. This was the first interventional use of liquid biopsy. Within the NO-CUT programme, circulating genetic and epigenetic biomarkers and the stromal transcriptome help interpret which patients with rectal cancer can safely avoid surgery after total neoadjuvant therapy. It is still unclear when a plasma result is solid enough to change treatment for a patient.

Since 2016 I have worked in the Niguarda Phase I Unit, as principal investigator and as sub-investigator, on studies conducted under the Italian AIFA 809/2015 framework. These include phase 2 and 3 studies as well as phase 1. Early-phase work is as much an organisational discipline as a scientific one: eligibility criteria that can actually be applied at the bedside, endpoints that the translational sampling can support, and enough operational capacity to run complex protocols safely. This is where my biostatistical training applies: sample size, estimands and analysis plans for the studies I design, and a critical reading of those I am asked to join.

---
title: Precision oncology in colorectal and gastrointestinal cancers
weight: 1
---

Colorectal cancer is no longer a single disease. HER2 amplification, KRAS G12C mutation, BRAF V600E and NTRK or ALK gene fusions each define a small population for which one specific drug may work far better than chemotherapy. Much of my clinical research has gone into bringing those agents to patients at Niguarda within first-in-human and phase 1b studies, and into reporting how they behave in practice: the phase 1b trial of the KRAS G12C inhibitor divarasib combined with cetuximab, the identification of a novel CAD-ALK rearrangement in colorectal cancer and its successful treatment with entrectinib, and a series of analyses of HER2 as both a therapeutic target and a predictor of resistance to anti-EGFR antibodies.

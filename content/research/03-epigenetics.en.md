---
title: Epigenetics and rechallenge strategies
weight: 3
---

One of the first lines of research at Niguarda concerned hypermethylation of the MGMT promoter as a predictor of response to alkylating agents in metastatic colorectal cancer. I designed and ran two single-centre phase 2 trials at Niguarda, within the European COLTHERES project and the MoTriColor consortium: the first showed that MGMT promoter hypermethylation predicts clinical response to dacarbazine, the second that MGMT methylation status shifts over time and that this drift limits the efficacy of temozolomide in colorectal tumours, even where there was initial susceptibility. From these studies and from the translational work that followed, we showed that temozolomide itself can alter mismatch-repair proteins and raise the mutational burden in the tumour and in the blood, which provides a rationale, within a clinical-trial setting, for sequencing alkylating agents with immunotherapy.

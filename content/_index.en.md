I graduated in Medicine and Surgery from the University of Pavia in 2005 with distinction, and completed my specialisation in Medical Oncology at the same university in 2009, also with distinction. After a first year at Fondazione S. Maugeri in Pavia, I moved to Niguarda in 2011 and have worked in its Department of Oncology and Haematology ever since.

I have been involved in independent clinical research from the outset, within the European Coltheres and MoTriColor projects. I have worked on phase 1 trials since 2016. In 2022 I was appointed to the High Specialisation post for Clinical Trials and Molecular Oncology, and in 2023 I took on the Molecular Tumor Board of the Medical Oncology Division. I run the gastrointestinal cancer outpatient clinics and take part in the multidisciplinary colorectal and anal cancer clinic.

Two postgraduate master's degrees in biostatistics for clinical research at the University of Padua, in 2019 and 2023, underpin the design and analysis of the studies I am responsible for. I am scientific director of the Niguarda residential course on phase 1 studies, and I teach clinical trial methodology on master's programmes at the University of Milan-Bicocca and the University of Milan. I supervise oncology residents from the University of Milan (La Statale) on the ward and in clinic.

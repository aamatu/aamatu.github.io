The primary results of NO-CUT, a multicentre, single-arm, phase 2 trial of total neoadjuvant therapy followed by non-operative management or surgery in stage II–III rectal cancer, appeared in The Lancet Oncology (2025;26(12):1612–1625). The study, funded by Fondazione AIRC and led by Salvatore Siena, combined clinical response assessment with circulating genetic and epigenetic biomarkers and the stromal transcriptome, to identify which patients can safely avoid surgery.

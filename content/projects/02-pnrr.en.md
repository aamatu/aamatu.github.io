Translational research programme built around the ALFEO trial and funded under the Italian National Recovery and Resilience Plan. It covers coordination of the bicentric study (Niguarda and Federico II, Naples) and the translational analyses: the molecular and cellular events triggered by high-dose vitamin C plus ipilimumab and nivolumab, predictive biomarkers of response, and the generation of patient-specific pre-clinical models. Principal investigator: Andrea Sartore-Bianchi.

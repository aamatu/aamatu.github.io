Modelling and predicting sensitivity to targeted therapies in colorectal cancer: a European multicentre project under the Seventh Framework Programme. It combined multi-omic profiling of patient samples, in vitro models to identify drug-resistance biomarkers, prospective screening of genes mediating resistance and sensitivity, and the design of biomarker-driven phase 2 studies. Principal investigator for Niguarda: Salvatore Siena. Within the project I was first author of three and co-author of five further peer-reviewed publications, including the two temozolomide and dacarbazine phase 2 trials I designed and ran at Niguarda.

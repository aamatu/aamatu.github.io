ALFEO, the phase 2 window-of-opportunity study of high-dose vitamin C added to neoadjuvant ipilimumab and nivolumab in mismatch repair-proficient colon cancer, has recently completed enrolment at Niguarda, which sponsors it, and I am the principal investigator. The trial sits inside the PNRR-funded translational programme PNRR-MAD-2022-12376593, which also covers the bicentric coordination with Federico II in Naples and the accompanying laboratory work.
